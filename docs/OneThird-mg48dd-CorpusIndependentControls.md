# CONTROLS G and H — closing two instances with references the corpus cannot produce (mg-48dd)

**What this is.** The landing of `mg-56be` finding 1. That audit's verdict on `mg-4f9b` was **RED**, and the
RED was **constructive**: it refuted a negative by exhibiting what the negative said could not exist. This
ticket implements two of the exhibits — `I1` and `I6` in the audit's numbering — as gate conditions
`CONTROL G` and `CONTROL H` in `scripts/onethird_mg2c34_n7_overlap_test.py`, and corrects the recorded
position from *"the gate cannot"* to *"the gate can, and here is how"*.

**The one-line result.** **Sixteen executable lines** (plus their docstrings, which are longer than they are
and say what the population is and why), no new imports, no new dataset, no new gate runs, on quantities the
gate already computes — and the two instances `mg-4f9b` recorded as open are now caught, **including the one
that survives regeneration**.

| | reference | closes | is the reference a number the corpus produced? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CONTROL G** | the integer **0**, from *a mean-centred vector sums to zero* | mg-bd53's `C2` — the instance that survived **four generations** | **no** |
| **CONTROL H** | the integer **1**, the vacuity floor `_projector_row_ok` already applies to `dim U` | mg-5ad1's `M3` **under regeneration** — the case mg-4f9b measured as exit 0 everywhere | **no** |

## §0 — What was wrong, and it was a framing error as much as a measurement error

`mg-4f9b` was asked for a diagnosis rather than a fourth widening, and produced one. Its §3.1 measurement is
correct, reproduces, and **stands**:

> **NO COMPARISON AGAINST THE STORED REFERENCE CAN CLOSE THE CLASS.** The identity check's reference is a
> frozen snapshot of the same code path; a mutation that moves the computation *and its own reference*
> together is invisible to any number of comparisons against that reference. Measured: `M3` is exit 1, `M3`
> with the eight rows the gate reads regenerated by the mutated code is exit **0**.

That is the most valuable thing the arc produced and nothing here touches it.

**What does not follow from it**, and what `mg-4f9b` wrote next:

> *"Can the gate close the class? **No.** … Where the class is detected: the independent audit stage … That
> is not a gap to be closed by this file; it is the division of labour."*

**A control need not be a comparison against a stored reference.** `mg-4f9b` evaluated exactly one
candidate — have the gate read its own committed output back — rejected it correctly, on the correct grounds
that a read-back only relocates the mutation into the regeneration row, and then generalised from that
single rejection to a negative about every possible control. It did not enumerate what it had tried, and it
was not required to.

**The licence for that was given, not taken.** pm-onethird told `mg-4f9b` to *"be willing to answer NO"* and
did not require it to say what it had considered. `mg-4f9b` then did the one thing that makes this
recoverable: it named its own negative as the audit's primary target, on the grounds that *"a 'no' is a
comfortable thing for an author to write because nothing can then be shown to have failed."* `mg-56be` took
the target and refuted it. **The refutation exists because an author asked to be checked on the one claim
that suited them**, and that is the part of this arc worth keeping.

## §1 — CONTROL G: the integer zero

```python
def _indicator_centred_ok(row, tag):
    v = row.get(f"{tag}_pair_indicator_sum")
    return v is None or abs(v) < INDICATOR_SUM_TOL      # 1e-9
```

`frozen_pair_indicator` centres `f` before normalising it, so `Σ_σ f(σ) = 0` — by arithmetic, not by
measurement. The reference is the integer `0`. Nothing in this repository can move it: no re-run of any
probe, no refreshed dataset, no author committing the numbers their edited code now produces.

**What it catches.** mg-bd53's `C2`: `f -= f.mean()` → `f -= 0.0 * f.mean()`. One line, in the gate's own
file. It moved `frozen_pair_overlap_with_U` — the quantity ledger claim 8 rests on — from
`0.807/0.809/0.810` to `0.904/0.908/0.905`, with the gate **printing the moved values and exiting 0** — on the
pinned pre-widening gate (`af7fc2df`, the mg-60d3 era) and on every revision since, up to and including
mg-4f9b's. Both columns of mg-bd53's own matrix, on both trees, in §5 below.

**My own counts** (`--part-a`, measured in-process, no gate runs):

| poset | `\|Σ f\|` baseline | `\|Σ f\|` under `C2` |
|---|---|---|
| `enum-n7-#3` | 2.220e-16 | **13.4164** |
| `enum-n7-#20` | 0.000e+00 | **10.0995** |
| `enum-n7-#600` | 2.220e-16 | **8.1240** |

It fires by roughly **sixteen orders of magnitude** against a tolerance of `1e-9`. There is no calibration
question here and no threshold anybody has to defend.

**Population.** Every measured poset, both pair selectors (`frozen` and `maxbias`). The identity is
universal, so the population is a **cost** choice and not a validity scope — the opposite of `CONTROL H`,
and the docstring says so, because a control whose stated reason is wider than its real one is this arc's
named defect.

## §2 — CONTROL H: the integer one, scoped

```python
def _overlap_proper_ok(row, tag):
    v = row.get(f"{tag}_pair_overlap_with_U")
    return v is None or v < 1.0 - OVERLAP_VACUITY_EPS   # 1e-6
```

This is the floor `_projector_row_ok` already applies to `dim U`, applied to the overlap itself. If the
frozen-pair indicator lies **in** `U`, the corpus's degree-2 inference (*"degree-2 slow mode ⇒ c ≈ 0"*) has
nothing left to be about and the printed `1.000000` carries no signal in either direction — sec 2's own
`null` criterion, one quantity along.

**What it catches, and why this row is the whole point.** mg-5ad1's `M3` — the Theorem-E frozen-pair selector
`min → max` — drives the overlap to **exactly** `1.000000` at 3 of 3 off-regime posets. The identity check
already catches `M3`. It stops catching it the moment the author who made that edit re-runs mg-8b64's probe
and commits the numbers it now produces, which is the ordinary workflow and which `mg-4f9b` measured as exit
**0** everywhere. `CONTROL H` does not stop, because it never reads the store.

**My own counts:**

| poset | overlap baseline | overlap under `M3` | `1 − overlap` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `enum-n7-#3` | 0.807168 | 1.000000 | 2.220e-15 |
| `enum-n7-#20` | 0.809421 | 1.000000 | −2.887e-15 |
| `enum-n7-#600` | 0.809650 | 1.000000 | 0.000e+00 |

**SCOPED, and here the scoping is load-bearing rather than a cost choice.** Off-regime measured posets only
(`enum-n7-#3/#20/#600`), `frozen` selector only. There are posets where `f ∈ U` **legitimately** — the gate's
own sweep counts them in `lemma_3_1_check` and asks whether they satisfy the lemma's threshold hypothesis —
so an unscoped floor would fire on correct input. Off-regime is where the corpus's own claim is that the
overlap is `~0.81` and emphatically not 1.

If it ever fires legitimately, **the fix is to narrow the population, not to loosen the number.** That is the
rule `_antichain_row_ok`'s docstring already states for `CONTROL B`, and it is stated again in
`_overlap_proper_ok`'s.

## §3 — Why neither can rot, stated in the code beside them

**Neither reference is a number the corpus produced.** `0` and `1` come from mathematics, not from a stored
row, so a mutation cannot move them with it and a regeneration cannot absorb them.

That is the **provenance axis satisfied rather than described**. `mg-4f9b` identified the axis, measured it,
named the design rule — *"a new control is worth more than a wider comparison exactly when its reference is
one the corpus cannot regenerate"* — and then did not apply the rule to the instance it had just recorded as
open. `mg-56be` did, in sixteen lines. The cost of the whole repair is smaller than the cost of the
sentence explaining why it could not be done.

## §6 — HONEST SCOPE, and this section is not a formality

**What is closed.** `frozen_pair_overlap_with_U` — the quantity `mg-4f9b` names as the open instance, and the
quantity ledger claim 8 rests on. Both named instances, `C2` and `M3`-under-regeneration, and both under
regeneration.

**What is NOT closed.** **The one-character-selector class remains open.** A selector flip that lands on a
legitimately-different pair *without* driving the overlap to 1 and *without* touching the centring passes
both new controls. `mg-4f9b`'s diagnosis of why the general class is hard remains the right one, because it
is a statement about comparisons against the store and both new controls are outside that statement — which
is exactly why they close anything at all, and also exactly why they do not close everything.

**What is refuted** is the specific proposition that *the gate cannot* close this class and must hand it to
the audit stage. It can, cheaply, with theorem-grade references. That is the difference between a residual
and a division of labour.

**A landing that said "class closed" would be the sixth generation of this arc's signature defect, in the
commit that finally fixed something.** The outcome record, updated:

```
    mg-09ea  AUDIT     two controls passing while blind        CAUGHT
    mg-60d3  gate fix  repaired those two instances            class survived
    mg-5ad1  AUDIT     one-character selector flip passes      CAUGHT
    mg-75f0  gate fix  widened 4 fields -> the whole row       class survived
    mg-bd53  AUDIT     a REGRESSION, and the class still open  CAUGHT

    mg-56be  AUDIT     the "gate CANNOT" negative is refuted   CAUGHT
```

The audit stage is 4 for 4. **The gate is still 0 for 3 on the class.** What changed is the last column of
the last row: previous gate fixes ended with an author believing the class was closed, and this one ends with
two named instances closed and the class recorded as open — which is a smaller claim, and it is the one that
is true.
